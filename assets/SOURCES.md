## November 2026 academic redesign

- Design and interaction reference: [Zhouhu Tan's public homepage](https://tanzhouhu.github.io/#research). No personal copy, code, photographs or video from that site is included here. The particle lettering and accessible research map are original local JavaScript/HTML/CSS implementations.
- The optional [mechanical iris study](../previews/iris/) uses original procedural artwork from `scripts/render-iris.py`: nine pivoting leaf edges, a polygonal aperture, an engraved bezel and a stationary microtype field. The H.264 film and opening/settled WebP stills are under `motion/iris*`; the homepage continues to load the arm study.
- `folio/fold-{1,2,3}.webp` are frames at 1.5, 5 and 9 seconds of `YUBI/factory/8-12/Fold_Box/foldbox.mp4`. The first is a wide crop, the other two show the same working area. The SHA-256 of the recording matches the [public file manifest](https://github.com/yzh423/factory-dataset/blob/main/metadata/files.csv).
- `folio/path-manual.webp` and `folio/path-research.webp` are equal-scale plotting-area crops of the Manual PID and Research PID panels at 00:09 of [the path-command recording](manipulation-research/r3-path-evidence.mp4). Both depict the same task at the recorded simulation time 2.670 s.
- The homepage robot arm film (`motion/grasp-study.mp4`) and opening/settled posters (`motion/grasp-study-opening.webp`, `motion/grasp-study.webp`) were procedurally drawn for this site using a two-link arm, parallel gripper, machined bushing and original print marks in `scripts/render-grasp.py`. The silent ten-second 1440 × 640, 60 fps H.264 film uses an original 3 × 5 uppercase alphabet, two-link inverse kinematics and a timed pickup, a macro camera move and copper impressions at the contacts. No reference frames, fonts or third-party footage were incorporated. It is decorative, not simulation evidence. Fold Box and control recordings keep their original files under the corresponding project disclosures.

Newsreader regular and italic variable fonts and license: [Google Fonts / Newsreader](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/newsreader). Downloaded from the official Google Fonts repository; locally compressed to WOFF2 with FontTools, preserving the source TTF and OFL license files.
