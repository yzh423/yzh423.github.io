About Me

Prospective MS / PhD applicant in robotics and AI

Building reliable embodied intelligence.

I am Zhenghao (Daniel) Yu, a Computer Engineering undergraduate at The University of Hong Kong. My work spans robot system design, learning-based control, autonomous driving, embedded systems, and human-computer interaction. I am interested in systems that remain measurable and useful outside a clean lab setting.

Most recently, at Ragtime Lab, I worked across the robotics evaluation stack: curating multimodal factory data, comparing robot designs, optimizing dual-arm mounting and trajectory execution, and building reproducible control studies. This systems perspective complements my earlier research in safety-critical driving, wheel-leg navigation, and contactless interaction for harsh vehicle cabins.

I like research that ends in auditable evidence: documented datasets, reproducible experiments, explicit acceptance criteria, and results that state their limits as clearly as their gains.

Game-Diffusion-based Multi-Agent Learning of Ramp Merging Coordination for Connected and Automated Vehicles

Zhenghao Yu, Chaoyi Chen, Nuo Lei, Bingbing Li, Boli Chen, and Hao Zhang

Game-Diff MARL combines diffusion-based trajectory refinement, game-theoretic strategy selection, and adaptive gating for robust CAV coordination in HighwayEnv. It reduces collision rate from 12.3% to 3.7% against the reported baselines.

Zhenghao Yu

Design and Wizard-of-Oz Evaluation of a Tri-Modal Contactless In-Vehicle HMI for High-Noise and High-Vibration Cabins
